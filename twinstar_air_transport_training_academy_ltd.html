About Twinstar Academy

Pioneering Excellence in Aviation, Travel & Tourism Training

TWINSTAR AIR TRANSPORT TRAINING ACADEMY LTD (RC NO: 201894) is a premier aviation institute in Nigeria dedicated to producing world-class travel agency practitioners, flight booking specialists, and travel consultants.

Equipped with practical Global Distribution Systems (GDS) computer labs in both our Kano Head Office and Kaduna Branch, we equip students with hands-on expertise in Amadeus, Galileo, and BSP Link Operations.

Our Core Mission

Committing to earning your trust by providing top-tier, industry-approved travel and transport education.

Career Readiness

Direct career placement support in airline offices, travel management companies, and tour agencies.
